Frequently asked questions

How do I color?

Choose a color or gradient from the palette at the bottom, then tap any white area of the picture. It fills inside the lines. Pinch to zoom or use the + and − buttons for small areas.

How do I fix a mistake?

Use Undo and Redo in the toolbar, or Reset to start the picture again.

How do I unlock the next picture?

Finish coloring every area of a picture, then tap Next level. You can also unlock any locked picture right away by watching an optional short video.

Why can I only color 3 pictures a day?

Color Splash is free and supported by ads. Each day you can color 3 different pictures for free. To color more that day, watch an optional short video for each extra picture. The count resets every day. Pictures you've already started today don't count again.

The video didn't load. What can I do?

Videos need an internet connection. Check your connection and try again in a moment. If no video is available, you can still color your free pictures for the day.

How do I save my picture?

Tap Save on the coloring screen. The first time, iOS asks for permission to add photos — choose Allow. If you declined earlier, go to Settings → Color Splash → Photos and choose Add Photos Only.

Will I lose my progress?

Your coloring is saved automatically on your device. Deleting the app removes saved progress, because nothing is stored on our servers.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law, a privacy choices button (gear icon) appears on the Themes screen — tap it to change your consent. To change tracking permission, go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
