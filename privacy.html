Color Splash: Paint Book

Effective date: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Color Splash: Paint Book app ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. The app is developed by Khizra Bajwa. By using the app you agree to this policy.

In short: we don't ask for your name, email, or any account. Your coloring stays on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may collect some device information to show and measure those ads, as described below.

1. Information the app stores on your device

  • Coloring progress — which pictures you've colored and the colors you used.
  • Daily picture count and unlocked pictures — used for the 3-free-pictures-per-day limit.

This information is stored only on your device. We do not receive it, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Photos

When you tap Save, the app asks for permission to add pictures to your photo library. The app can only add your finished artwork; it cannot see, read, or upload your existing photos. You can change this anytime in Settings → Color Splash → Photos.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and supported by ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). To show, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • Device identifiers — such as the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and app-specific identifiers.
  • Advertising data — such as ads you have seen or tapped.
  • Product interaction data — such as app launches and taps.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (not GPS).
  • Diagnostics — crash and performance data.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for teens (Google's "T" rating).

4. Your choices

  • Tracking: the app asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before the advertising identifier can be used. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland, and some US states): where required by law, Google's consent form is shown on first launch. You can change your choices later with the privacy choices (gear) button on the Themes screen.
  • Rewarded videos are optional. You never have to watch one.

5. Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app and Google has collected information, contact us and we will help you request deletion.

6. Data sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information ourselves. The only third party that receives data from the app is Google AdMob, as described in section 3.

7. Data retention and deletion

Everything the app stores is on your device; delete the app to remove it. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies. You can reset your advertising identifier or limit tracking in iOS Settings at any time.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to opt out of personalized advertising. Because we hold no personal data, most requests are handled by the settings above or by Google. You can still contact us and we will help.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
