A relaxing coloring book for teens and adults

Pick a color, tap a shape, and watch your picture come to life. Color Splash has 48 original pictures in four themes — Cars & Trucks, Cartoon Heroes, Dinosaurs, and Space & Robots.

  • Solid colors and smooth gradients
  • Zoom and pan for tiny details
  • Undo, redo, and reset
  • Progress saved automatically
  • Save to Photos and share

Available for iPhone and iPad. Free with ads.
